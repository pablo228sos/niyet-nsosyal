# Product thesis

## Distribution is not resolution

Social platforms are excellent at distributing content and attention. That does not guarantee that the need behind a post is resolved.

Two failures motivate DRSK:

1. **Evidence integrity failure.** A post can cite a real source and still state something stronger than the source supports.
2. **Attention routing failure.** A useful question can exist in the same network as a willing knowledgeable person and still never reach them.

DRSK treats both as resolution problems.

## The SOURCECHAIN wedge

SOURCECHAIN is deliberately narrower than a generic fact-checker.

> **Does this evidence actually support this exact social claim?**

A search engine can find documents. A chatbot can generate an answer about them. SOURCECHAIN preserves the claim, exact passage, provenance and relation together.

A real citation can create false confidence. For example:

```text
post:   research proves coffee causes lower mortality
source: coffee consumption was associated with lower mortality
```

The source is real. The wording is not equivalent. SOURCECHAIN can expose that as a claim/evidence conflict and a causality shift without pretending to have a universal truth score.

### Author-side value

Before publishing a factual claim, an author can privately check whether their wording overstates the evidence.

### Reader-side value

A reader may want evidence context for someone else's factual post. The target native interaction is an optional DRSK action attached to the social object. It does not mean every post is suspicious or automatically checked.

The current real NSosyal overlay proves the host integration and author/published-post lifecycle. The `/live` feed shows the native product concept and four resolution states. Injecting an action under every arbitrary foreign NSosyal DOM post remains future native-integration scope.

## NIYET's job

NIYET does not identify “the person who knows the truth.” It routes an explicit request to a responder who is relevant, willing, available and within attention capacity. Follower count is not an eligibility signal.

A human answer remains **human context**, not verified evidence.

## Why not just ask an LLM?

A generic chatbot gives another generated answer in another conversation. DRSK remains attached to the original social need and can expose exact evidence, decide that evidence is already enough, route a willing responder only when human context adds value, keep evidence and human context separate, and choose NONE when no intervention is useful.

DRSK is selective by design rather than generative by default.

## Why not just Google it?

Google is optimized to retrieve documents. SOURCECHAIN is optimized to compare a social claim with bounded evidence and make the exact passage and relationship inspectable.

## What `Resolved` means

Resolved does not mean absolute truth was established. It means the chosen resolution path completed for that social need.

- **EVIDENCE:** bounded evidence is enough.
- **HUMAN:** contextual or practical human input is useful.
- **BOTH:** evidence exposes a gap and human context adds value.
- **NONE:** the right product behavior is not to intervene.

## False claims and safe failure

For an arbitrary false claim, SOURCECHAIN can produce `CONFLICTING` when strong contradictory evidence is grounded, or `INSUFFICIENT` when it cannot establish the relationship reliably.

`INSUFFICIENT` does not mean true. It means the system refuses to manufacture a confident verdict.

## The product in one sentence

> **DRSK is a resolution layer for social platforms: SOURCECHAIN shows what the evidence actually supports, NIYET routes explicit unresolved needs to relevant willing people, and the Resolution Engine decides whether the interaction needs evidence, a human, both, or neither.**
